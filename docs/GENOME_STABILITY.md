# Genome stability is not a division-speed score

AI-assisted personal learning project. Primary-source reading is agent-run, not expert
review. Published-method parameters appear as cited research-reference recipes
([METHOD_RECIPES.md](METHOD_RECIPES.md)). Nothing here is a culture schedule, assay
protocol, gene-editing design, cancer-prevention advice, treatment instruction or
transplant-safety determination.

## The chemical question

There is no generic 'sprinkle chemicals over cells to repair their DNA' operation in this
workbench. Cellular copying, repair and checkpoint processes are not a protective coating.
Halliwell et al. (2020), DOI **10.1016/j.stemcr.2020.04.004**, PMC7355123,
Summary/Results/Figure 3, studied exogenous nucleosides in human PSC culture medium.
They reported reduced replication stress, genome-damage markers and mitotic aberrations.
This is evidence about measured endpoints in those cultures, not proof of universal DNA
repair, reversal of existing mutations, removal of selected mutant clones, prevention of
cancer or safety of an engineered graft. The study's published formulation and culture
conditions are restated as a cited research-reference card
([METHOD_RECIPES.md](METHOD_RECIPES.md)) — a description of what one study did, never
universal use instructions — and the app has no 'apply repair' or checkpoint-suppression
control.

## Different questions

- **Replication stress / damage markers:** endpoints in the Halliwell study, not a direct
  count of all inherited sequence changes or a cancer probability.
- **Mutation and culture selection:** Merkle et al. (2017), DOI **10.1038/nature22312**,
  PMC5427175, Results/Figure 3d, observed expansion of TP53-mutant hESC subpopulations
  across passages; Figure 4 includes broader hPSC screening. A selection advantage is
  not the same as counting every replication event. Not every screened line had a
  detectable mutation, and this app does not estimate an individual's cancer risk.
- **Senescence:** the fibroblast observation in Sturm et al. is a culture-specific plateau,
  not an indefinite exponential expansion law or universal passage limit.
- **Identity, contamination, residual pluripotent cells and phenotype/function:** distinct
  unanswered quality questions. Choosing a profile or completing a virtual gate does not
  measure them or certify absence of tumor-forming cells.

Published sequencing, allele-fraction and damage-marker observations answer different
questions. No single endpoint in this workbench certifies a complete genome, every cell in
a heterogeneous population, long-term stability or clinical suitability. Real laboratory
results are not connected. The evidence ledger records source endpoints and limitations;
it is not a validated laboratory checklist and has no acceptance thresholds.
## Adverse cross-species evidence ships beside benefit claims

Kurashima et al. (2024), DOI 10.1038/s44319-024-00207-5, PMC11387781, report that forced
replication-fork acceleration with nucleosides in mouse ES cells miscoordinated
replication completion from cell-cycle progression, increasing ultra-fine bridges (about
20% to about 40% of cells), micronuclei and gamma-H2AX (6.1% to 13.1%). The same
intervention class therefore carries adverse mouse evidence alongside the human
Halliwell endpoints. Species, cell state, concentration and conditions differ; no
cross-species transfer or contradiction claim is made. The method-evidence panel and
every related recipe card display this adverse evidence. See
[REPAIR_METHODS.md](REPAIR_METHODS.md) and [METHOD_RECIPES.md](METHOD_RECIPES.md).
