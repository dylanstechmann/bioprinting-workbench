# Whole-organ aspirations: an evidence-gap map

AI-assisted personal hobby and learning project; agent-read sources, not independent
domain review. No laboratory fabrication, patient/animal-directed procedure or
whole-organ production forecast is supplied.

A tissue patch, organoid, assembloid, decellularized scaffold and complete functional
organ are not equivalent. The displayed scaffold is original geometric artwork. The
independent oxygen box is not liver transport and the resistance graph is not living
sinusoidal vasculature. Neither is coupled to cell division.

Yuan et al., **Human assembloids recapitulate periportal liver tissue in vitro**,
DOI **10.1038/s41586-025-09884-1**, PMC12893922: online December 17, 2025, journal
issue February 2026. This is a periportal tissue model, not a complete manufactured liver.
The Abstract/Introduction describe hepatocyte, cholangiocyte and portal-fibroblast
compartments. The Discussion identifies absent other mesenchymal cells, immune cells
and portal vasculature as limitations. Human in-vitro findings and reported animal
experiments are distinct endpoints, neither a ready-to-use human organ.

The app's dependency map is **authored teaching organization**, not a published or proven
fabrication order. Every barrier remains 'not modeled': identity/maturity, stability,
architecture/mechanical support, blood supply/transport, biliary drainage/organization,
immune/stromal context and long-term function. Liver zonation, multiple transport routes
and long-term maintenance are questions, not implemented mechanisms. Generic endothelial
cells, MSCs and liver-specific subtypes are not interchangeable on demand.

## What does a seven-year horizon mean?

It is a user-selected research-planning annotation only. It does not accelerate expansion,
establish an experiment duration, certify any barrier resolved, or calculate an organ
completion date. The reviewed work does not establish that a human liver can be made in
seven years, or any other manufacturing duration. Waiting alone is not modeled as
solving any biological dependency. Other organs and body parts are explicitly unevaluated
aspirations rather than automatic extensions of the liver comparison.

The annotation can be saved in scenario /3, but no organ mass target, success probability,
readiness percentage, treatment or surgical action is exported.
