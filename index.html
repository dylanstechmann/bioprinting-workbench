THE MODEL IS NOT THE TISSUE

Real research. Explicit boundaries.

This scaffold-first storyboard brings together concepts from distinct research workflows. It is not a reproduction of either paper and does not model a complete organ.

01 / PRINTED TISSUES

Cells, matrix & perfusable channels

Kolesky et al. (2016) demonstrated multimaterial printing of thick vascularized tissues. Our geometry is original and our animation does not reproduce their experiments.

02 / VASCULAR ARCHITECTURE

More than a red tube

Grigoryan et al. (2019) explored multivascular hydrogel networks using a different printing approach. A tracer moving through a channel does not establish living vessel function.

Read the primary paper ↗
Model, assets & missing biology

All geometry is original procedural artwork, inspired by generic enclosed printers, culture equipment and perfusion setups. Three.js 0.187.0 (MIT) is bundled locally. No patient data, network APIs, analytics or vendor models are used. Missing: oxygen transport, fluid dynamics, cell mechanics, endothelial function, immune response, contamination, scaffold degradation, host anastomosis and healing. The culture, construct and recipient views use different illustrative scales. See methods, source ledger and asset/import guidance.
