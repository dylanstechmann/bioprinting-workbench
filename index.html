    Model, assets & missing biology

    All geometry is original procedural artwork, inspired by generic enclosed printers, culture equipment and perfusion setups. Three.js 0.187.0 (MIT) is bundled locally. No patient data, network APIs, analytics or vendor models are used. The oxygen slab is a separate analytical teaching model, not coupled to the construct. Missing: 3D oxygen transport, fluid dynamics, cell mechanics, endothelial function, immune response, contamination, scaffold degradation, host anastomosis and healing. The culture, construct and recipient views use different illustrative scales. See methods, source ledger and asset/import guidance.
